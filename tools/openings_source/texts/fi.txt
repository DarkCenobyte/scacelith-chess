# Coach openings: Finnish texts (keys of en.txt). tools/gen_opening_lang.py merges them with the names of
# families.json into assets/coach/openings/fi.lang.
# The coach says "sinä" (standard Finnish, never colloquial forms). Opening names stay in the nominative in
# every template ("def" = the citation name in lower case except proper names: "sisilialainen puolustus",
# "Najdorfin muunnelma"): they follow "on", "laudalla on" or a colon, so no case form is needed.
# Squares are never inflected ("ruutuun e5", "ruudussa f3", "e5-sotilas", "siirrolla c5"), since the voice
# spells them; files as "c-linja", pawns as "d-sotilas" (the file_pattern spells the letter); pieces in
# words, never piece letters. Numbers in words ("toisella siirrolla").
