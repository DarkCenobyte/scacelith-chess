# Coach openings: Czech texts (keys of en.txt). tools/gen_opening_lang.py merges them with the names of
# families.json into assets/coach/openings/cs.lang.
# Standard Czech, the coach's register: informal "ty" ("hraješ", "tvoje zahájení"). The coach is male; the
# player's gender is unknown: no second-person past tense or conditional, the present instead ("hraješ ho",
# "ty ho přijímáš"). "bílý" / "černý" = White / Black as players: masculine (grammar, not the player's
# gender), lower case mid-sentence as in Czech chess books.
# Names appear only in the nominative ("def" = the lower-case nominative, "sicilská obrana"; names made from a
# person's name keep their capital: "Najdorfova varianta", "Caro-Kannova obrana"): after "je", after a colon,
# or as the subject. In "declined" / "accepted" White's opening is always a gambit (dámský / královský gambit,
# masculine inanimate, accusative = nominative), and "ho" refers back to it.
# Composed lichess names (opening.compose.*): Czech names a variation after a person with a possessive
# adjective whose ending agrees with the noun ("Steinitzova varianta", "Steinitzův gambit", "Steinitzovo
# zahájení"), so the eponyms' "gen" form in the names fragment holds the stem those endings attach to
# ("Steinitz", "Capablanc"), and only eponyms compose (compose_proper_names = false).
# Squares as "e4"; files as "sloupec c" and pawns as "pěšec d" (the file_pattern of the fragment spells those
# letters out; a pawn letter is never "a", the conjunction); pieces in words, no piece letters.
