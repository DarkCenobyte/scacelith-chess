# Coach openings: Brazilian Portuguese texts (keys of en.txt). tools/gen_opening_lang.py merges them with the
# names of the pt-BR fragment of families.json into assets/coach/openings/pt-BR.lang.
# Register: the coach says "você" (never "tu"), "seu / sua"; the coach is male. Brazilian chess terms (glossary
# pt-BR): lance, casa, coluna, fileira, ala do rei / ala da dama, fianqueto, ruptura, par de bispos, "as Brancas"
# and "as Pretas" (capitalised, feminine plural) for the players.
# The "def" form of every name starts with its article ("a Defesa Siciliana", "o Gambito da Dama"): templates put
# it only after a verb or after a preposition that does not contract (com, para, contra), never after de, em, a
# or por ("da", "na", "à", "pela" would be needed). Agreement with an implicit name ("Objetivamente duvidoso")
# follows the family's gender (Defesa, Abertura f; Gambito, Ataque, Sistema m).
# Squares as "e4"; files as "coluna c", pawns as "peão d" (the fragment's file_pattern spells the letter for the
# voice): never write "peão a mais" or "peão e o ..." (the letter would be spelled). Pieces in words.
