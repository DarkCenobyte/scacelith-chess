# Coach openings: Italian texts (keys of en.txt). tools/gen_opening_lang.py merges them with the names of
# families.json into assets/coach/openings/it.lang. The generator applies no Italian typography: write the
# typographic apostrophe (’) and « » here directly.
# The coach says "tu" and is male; nothing agrees with the player's gender (avere past tenses: "hai aperto",
# "hai scelto"; "abbiamo lasciato la teoria" rather than "siamo usciti").
# Names: citation forms in sentence case ("Difesa siciliana", "Gambetto di donna"); the "def" forms carry the
# article and keep the head noun in lower case ("la difesa siciliana", "il gambetto di donna", "la variante
# Najdorf"). The templates put a name only after a verb, after "è" or after "con" / "per", never after di, a, da,
# in, su (they would fuse with the article). In the comments, elliptical names are capitalised as Italian chess
# writing does ("la Siciliana", "la Spagnola", "l’Italiana", "la Caro-Kann"); full names keep the lower-case
# head noun ("il gambetto Evans", "l’attacco Marshall").
# declined / accepted: {white} there is always a gambit (il gambetto di donna / di re, masculine): "l’ho rifiutato".
# White and Black as players: "il Bianco", "il Nero" (capitalised); "a colori invertiti" = with the colours
# reversed; "la grande diagonale" = the long diagonal; "pedone isolato di donna" = isolated d-pawn (spoken
# "pedone d" would sound like "pedone di").
# Squares as "e4", files as "la colonna c", pawns as "il pedone d"; pieces in words, never piece letters. The
# voice spells the file letters b c d f g h after "colonna"/"colonne"/"pedone"/"pedoni" (file_pattern of the
# names fragment); the files a and e sound the same as the words, so they need no spelling. Never write two
# file letters joined by "e" ("le colonne a e b"): write "la colonna a e la colonna b".
