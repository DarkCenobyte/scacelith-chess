# Coach openings: Danish texts (keys of en.txt). tools/gen_opening_lang.py merges them with the names of
# families.json into assets/coach/openings/da.lang.
# Register: the coach says "du" to the player and "jeg" of himself; nothing in Danish agrees with the player's
# gender. The sides are "hvid" / "sort" in lower case in running text (capital at the start of a sentence).
# Names in sentences ("def"): an adjective name, a gambit and a "-spil" / "-parti" name are used bare, as
# Danish players say them ("siciliansk forsvar", "dronninggambit", "firspringerspil"); a compound ending in
# -system, -angreb, -variant, -forsvar or -åbning takes the suffixed article ("London-systemet",
# "Najdorf-varianten", "Pirc-forsvaret"); an adjective variation takes "den" ("den klassiske variant"); the
# generic families take "en" ("en kongebondeåbning"). Comments use the short names Danish players say
# ("siciliansk", "spansk", "fransk", "kongeindisk"). A compound with a proper name takes a hyphen.
# Squares as "e4", files as "c-linjen", pawns as "d-bonden" (the voice spells them through file_pattern);
# pieces in words, never piece letters. Quotation marks » «. No comma before an at/som/der/hvis clause that
# follows its main clause (no "startkomma").

# ---- Sentences. "def" = the form used inside a sentence (see above). The gambit sentences say "den" for the
# gambit (en gambit). {line} is a citation form without article (after a colon).
