# Coach openings: Dutch texts (keys of en.txt). tools/gen_opening_lang.py merges them with the names of
# families.json into assets/coach/openings/nl.lang.
# Register: the coach says "je" ("jij" / "jouw" for a contrast) and "ik" / "mijn" for himself; nothing in Dutch
# agrees with the player's gender. Opening names are common nouns in running text ("het damegambiet", "de
# ruilvariant"); only proper-name parts keep their capital ("de Siciliaanse verdediging", "de Najdorf-variant").
# A compound with a proper name takes a hyphen (Najdorf-variant, Evans-gambiet), as the composed names do
# ("{X}-variant"). Comments may use the short names Dutch players say ("het Siciliaans", "het Frans").
# Squares as "e4", files as "c-lijn", pawns as "d-pion" (the voice spells them through file_pattern); pieces in
# words, never piece letters. A move is never put after an adjective ("een vroeg h6"): Dutch has no gender for it.

# sentences say "het" for the gambit (het gambiet). {line} is a citation form without article (after a colon).
