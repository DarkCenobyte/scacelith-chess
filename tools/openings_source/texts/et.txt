# Coach openings: Estonian texts (keys of en.txt). tools/gen_opening_lang.py merges them with the names of
# families.json into assets/coach/openings/et.lang.
# Register: the coach says "sina" ("sa", "sinu", singular imperatives) and "ma" / "minu" for himself; Estonian
# has no grammatical gender, so nothing agrees with the sex of the coach or of the player.
# Opening names stand only in the nominative (after "on", after a colon, "laual on"): the "def" forms of the names
# fragment are the names as written inside a sentence, lower case except proper-name parts ("lipugambiit",
# "kuningaindia kaitse", "Sitsiilia kaitse", "Najdorfi variant"). The gambit sentences say "selle" for the gambit.
# White and Black are "valge" and "must" (singular nouns, lower case): "valge ründab", "musta kuningas", "valgel on".
# Squares as "e4", never with a case ending: the case goes on a noun next to them ("väljal e4", "väljale f6",
# "käiguga c5", "ettur e5", "läbimurre d4"). Files as "c-vertikaal", pawns as "d-ettur" (the voice spells the
# letter through file_pattern); pieces in words, never piece letters. Numbers in words ("üheksateistkümnes").

# ---- Sentences. "pw" = the player (the listener) has White, "pb" = the player has Black; the coach is the
# other side. {line}: a composed lichess variation name (nominative, after a colon).
