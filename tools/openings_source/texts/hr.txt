# Coach openings: Croatian texts (keys of en.txt). tools/gen_opening_lang.py merges them with the names of
# families.json into assets/coach/openings/hr.lang.
# Standard Croatian (ijekavian), the coach's register: informal "ti". The coach is male ("odbio sam"); the
# player's gender is unknown: no second-person past tense or conditional, the present instead ("ti ga
# prihvaćaš"). "bijeli" / "crni" = White / Black as players, masculine, lower case mid-sentence.
# Names appear only in the nominative: after "je" (copula), after a colon, or as the subject. The "def" form of
# a name is its citation form with a lower-case first letter, except proper adjectives and names ("Najdorfova
# varijanta", "Caro-Kann obrana"). In "declined" / "accepted" the name is always a gambit (masculine), and
# "ga" refers back to it. Possessive adjectives in the long forms ("kraljevog pješaka", "na daminom krilu").
# Composed lichess names (opening.compose.*): Croatian names a variation after a person with a possessive
# adjective whose ending agrees with the noun ("Steinitzova varijanta", "Steinitzov napad", "Steinitzovo
# otvaranje"), so the eponyms' "gen" form in the names fragment holds that possessive stem ("Steinitzov"),
# and only eponyms compose (compose_proper_names = false).
# Squares as "e4"; files as "linija c" and pawns as "pješak d" (the file_pattern of the fragment spells those
# letters out); pieces in words.
