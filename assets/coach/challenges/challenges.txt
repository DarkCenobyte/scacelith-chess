#   challenge <id> <group> <level>
#       A set, in menu order. <group>: mates, tactics, defence, endgames. <level>: difficulty 1..5.
#       Texts: challenge.<id>.name / .desc / .detail in assets/i18n; ch.intro.<id> and
#       ch.task.<id> in assets/coach/speech/<lang>/challenge.lang.
#   line <source> <rating> | <fen> | <lead> | <moves> [| also <k>=<uci>,<uci> ...]
#       A position with a known solution. <lead>: the coach's move into it (Black, UCI), "-" when
#       White moves at once. <moves>: the solution from White's move on, the coach's answers in
#       between (UCI), ending with White's move. also: other moves accepted at the player's k-th
#       move (0-based; only at the last one: the positions have one solution up to there).
#   play <source> | <fen> | mate|promote|hold [<moves>]
#       An endgame played out against Stockfish, White to move: checkmate the coach, promote a pawn
